Sources and rules used

  • Money: FEC candidate H2MA09072 (House) and S0MA00083 (2013 Senate). Totals are for the full election cycle; 2026 runs through Aug 12, 2026.
  • Donation sizes are the FEC's own buckets, per donor per cycle. Donations of $200 or less aren't itemized, so the FEC counts them as one group.
  • PAC types come from the FEC's record of each PAC's sponsor (labor union, corporation or cooperative, trade or membership group). ActBlue and other conduits are left out of top backers because they pass along individuals' donations.
  • "Maxed": a PAC can give $5,000 per election, so $10,000 covers both the primary and the general.
  • Outside money: FEC-processed independent expenditures. Late 24- and 48-hour reports can take weeks to appear in these totals.
  • Vote counts: Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth election statistics.
